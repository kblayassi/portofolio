Privacy Policy

Case Closed · Last updated: October 2026

Data controller

The Case Closed app is published by Kevin Blayassi. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data involved

Case Closed saves your game progress: solved cases, stars, best times, number of hints used, games in progress and your settings. No account, email address or identity information is required to play.

Storage

This data is stored only on your device. It is never sent to a server run by the publisher, nor sold or shared with third parties.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your achievement progress to Apple (for example “10 cases solved”). This information is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; the publisher has no access to it. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent and the game works normally.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising, no trackers and no third-party analytics.

Payments

Case packs are one-time in-app purchases, processed by Apple through the App Store. The publisher never receives or stores your payment details (see Apple's privacy policy).

Contacting the developer

The “Contact the developer” button opens your email app. If you write, the publisher receives your message and your email address, used only to reply to you.

Young audiences

Case Closed asks for no personal information and collects no data for advertising. The cases involve criminal investigations (and, in the Halloween special edition, ghosts) without any violent imagery.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on the device for as long as the app is installed. You can erase everything from the game’s settings (“Erase all progress”) or by uninstalling the app; your purchases can always be recovered with “Restore purchases”.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data. Since your data stays on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the app evolves. The version in force is the one published on this page.

Contact

For any question about this policy, you can write to contact@kevinblayassi.dev.
