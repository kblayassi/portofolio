Privacy Policy

PokoCheck · Last updated: June 2026

Your privacy matters. PokoCheck is designed to work without a personal account and without collecting your data. This page explains how your information is handled.

No data collection

PokoCheck collects no personal data. The app records no usage statistics, generates no crash reports and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising tools.

No third-party services or network access

PokoCheck includes no third-party services, no ad networks and no tracking tools. The app does not send your information to any external server.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences and progress data are stored locally on your device so that the app can work. This data stays on your device and is never passed on to third parties.

iCloud sync (optional)

PokoCheck may offer optional iCloud sync. When you turn it on, your progress data is synced between your devices through your personal iCloud account, managed by Apple. This data is encrypted and accessible only to you; PokoCheck has no access to it outside your device.

In-app purchases

PokoCheck offers a one-time purchase to unlock all of the content. Transactions are handled entirely by Apple according to your App Store account settings. PokoCheck has no access to your payment information.

Children

Since PokoCheck collects no personal data, it gathers no information about children or any other user.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves and new features are introduced. Any change will be published on this page.

Contact

For any question about this policy, you can write to contact@kevinblayassi.dev.

Legal notice

PokoCheck is an unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored or approved by the publisher of the game Pokopia.
