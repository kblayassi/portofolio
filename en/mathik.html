About

Mathik is a native iPhone and iPad app that turns practicing high-school math drills into a daily game. Following the French national curriculum (Seconde and Première, roughly 10th and 11th grade), it offers short mental math sessions: every question is solved in your head, with a correction that always explains the method, never just the answer. A spaced-repetition engine brings topics back at the right time so they stick for good. Guided by Mathik, the π mascot, you progress at your own pace — with levels, streaks and badges along the way. The app is available in French only.

Features

Short drill sessions, solved in your head
Spaced repetition for long-term memory
French curriculum: Seconde & Première
Corrections that teach mental math methods
Lesson cards: method + example for each topic
Levels, streaks, badges and mastery tracking
Customizable π mascot (colors & personalities)
Friend codes to compare progress
iCloud sync across devices
