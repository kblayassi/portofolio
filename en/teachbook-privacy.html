Privacy Policy

TeachBook · Last updated: June 2026

Data controller

The TeachBook app is published by Kevin Blayassi. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data involved

The app lets you enter and organize: your classes, your students (last name, first name and, optionally, date of birth, contact details, legal guardians, accommodations, photo), grades, assessments, observations, timetables, appointments and documents (PDF).

Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device and, if iCloud is turned on, synced through your private iCloud database (CloudKit). The publisher has no access to this data: it stays in your personal iCloud space, managed by Apple. It is never collected on a server run by the publisher, passed on or sold to third parties.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising, no trackers and no third-party analytics.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The publisher never receives or stores your payment details (see Apple's privacy policy).

Permissions

Photos: used only when you choose to add an image (a student photo or a wallpaper); the app then accesses the images you select.
Notifications: used for appointment reminders, if you turn them on.

Student data (minors)

As a teacher, you are the controller of the student data you enter. You must have a legal basis and comply with the applicable regulations (GDPR) and your school's rules. The publisher only provides the tool and does not access this data.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you keep it in the app. You can delete it at any time in the app, and erase everything by uninstalling the app and deleting the associated data in iCloud.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data. Since your data stays under your control in your iCloud, you can exercise these rights directly in the app and in your iCloud account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the app evolves. The version in force is the one published on this page.

Contact

For any question about this policy, you can write to contact@kevinblayassi.dev.
