About

Case Closed is a native deduction game for iPhone and iPad. A crime has just been committed: every suspect was standing on one cell of the floor plan, and each one has a clue — “I was sitting in the armchair”, “I never set foot in the kitchen”… Only one person per row and per column, and the victim takes the last free cell: work out who was where, then unmask the culprit, the only suspect in the same room as the victim. Every case has a single solution, reached by pure deduction. Inspector Bolt walks you through three tutorials, then 22 cases to learn the trade, before five big investigations and two special editions for Halloween and Christmas.
