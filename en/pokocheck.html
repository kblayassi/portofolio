About

PokoCheck is a companion app designed to track your progress in a clear, simple and elegant way. Complete your Pokédex, browse over 1,300 collectibles and items, check handy guides and customize your progress widget. The free version lets you try the app, and a one-time lifetime purchase unlocks all of the content.

Features

A full Pokédex to complete
Over 1,300 collectibles and items
Customizable progress widgets
Habitats, locations and events
Recipes and cooking guides
Share your progress as an image
Unofficial fan app · not affiliated with the game's publisher
