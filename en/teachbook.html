About

TeachBook is a native iPadOS app, with an iPhone companion app, designed by and for teachers. Keep your gradebook with averages and weightings calculated automatically, plan your week in a planner you can annotate with Apple Pencil, build seating charts with simple drag and drop, and follow each student's progress with clear charts. Reports and profiles export to PDF, ready to share, and everything syncs across your devices with iCloud. The app is free to try, with a subscription to unlock every feature.

Features

Gradebook with automatic averages
Planner and weekly timetable
Apple Pencil annotations
Drag-and-drop seating charts
Progress charts for each student
Reports and profiles exported to PDF
Observations, tasks and notes
iCloud sync
iPad + iPhone companion
